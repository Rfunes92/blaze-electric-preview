While you wait

Electrical emergency? Three things to do before we arrive

STEP 1

Keep clear

Don't touch a sparking outlet, a hot cover plate, or anything near water.

STEP 2

Cut the power if it's safe

Switch off the main breaker or the affected circuit. If the panel is wet or smoking, leave it alone.

STEP 3

Call Blaze

Tell us what you see and smell. We'll guide you on the phone and send help.
